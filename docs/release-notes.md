Papan for Android 0.3.0 adds independent phone collections and browsing/saving to open collections in [Papan desktop 0.3.3+](https://github.com/chelij/papan/releases/tag/v0.3.3).

- **Collect on your phone.** Create collections, paste or share a link into a chosen destination, cache previews, edit references and retry failed capture. Phone collections work without pairing.
- **Explore aligned previews.** Edge-to-edge rows retain desktop frame proportions with adjustable sizing, zoom/crop filling and lighter single-line captions. Open fitted full-screen videos/images, zoom images and swipe between pins and attachments. Scrolling hides the top and bottom bars together; scrolling upward brings them back smoothly.
- **Use compact native controls.** Choose collections beside the Papan wordmark. One bottom-left button switches Phone/Desktop/Activity, using green highlights and a menu that reveals above its button. Controls retain Android tap targets, scalable labels and keyboard access. Light/Dark/System appearance is available.
- **Browse and save to desktop.** Enable phone browsing on desktop and choose an open collection when saving. Phone previews follow desktop order, including rearrangements, and reuse decoded thumbnails. Protected collections require their initial password on the phone; switching collections and rotating retain separate grants until app close or desktop lock/revocation.
- **Pair nearby and recover addresses.** Choose a nearby computer, compare its six-digit code and approve on desktop. Verify the paired desktop before accepting a new IP address or receiver port. QR/manual pairing remains available; queued links retain their chosen destination.
